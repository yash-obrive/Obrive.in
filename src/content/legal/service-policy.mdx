<CompanyInfoSection title="Customer-Facing Service Delivery, Scope, Engagement & Support Policy">
  <p><strong>Effective Date:</strong> 15 September 2026</p>
  <p>This Service Policy (“Policy”) establishes the general commercial and operational framework under which Obrive Industries Private Limited (“Obrive”, “Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) provides professional services to customers. It applies together with the applicable quotation, proposal, statement of work, purchase order, master service agreement, retainer agreement, subscription/order form, invoice and other written commercial documents (“Contract”). If a signed Contract contains service-specific terms that differ from this Policy, the Contract will govern to the extent of the difference.</p>
</CompanyInfoSection>

<CompanyInfoSection title="Overview">
  <p>Obrive provides connected digital product, technology, AI, design, development, commerce, integration, content, search-discovery and immersive technology services. Our service model is designed around a practical principle: understand the customer’s business objective, users, workflows, information, technology environment and desired outcome, then define the appropriate scope, delivery approach and responsibilities.</p>
  <p>This Policy is intended to make the customer relationship clear before and during delivery. It explains how scope, approvals, customer responsibilities, timelines, resources, revisions, testing, deployment, support, third-party dependencies, recurring services, payments, intellectual property and service changes are generally handled.</p>
  <p>The Policy does not promise a particular commercial result unless that result is expressly stated as a contractual obligation in writing.</p>
</CompanyInfoSection>

<CompanyInfoSection title="Service Engagement Model">
  <p>Every engagement begins with an agreed commercial scope. Depending on the project, Obrive may provide a discovery phase, proposal, statement of work, milestone plan, retainer plan or other written service definition.</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Scope is determined by the approved Contract and associated documents.</li>
    <li>Services not expressly included in the agreed scope are treated as additional work.</li>
    <li>Obrive may recommend phased delivery where a requirement is complex, uncertain or dependent on discovery.</li>
    <li>Where requirements are not sufficiently defined, discovery, research or technical assessment may be required before final implementation scope is confirmed.</li>
    <li>Customer requests made after approval may require a change request, revised fee or revised timeline.</li>
  </ul>
</CompanyInfoSection>

<CompanyInfoSection title="Scope & Change Management">
  <p>The agreed scope is the basis for estimating effort, resources, timeline and fees. A request that changes functionality, design direction, platform, content volume, integrations, technology, device support, data requirements or acceptance criteria may be treated as a scope change.</p>
  <ul>
    <li>New features or functionality may require additional development effort.</li>
    <li>Additional design directions or revision rounds may be chargeable where they exceed the agreed scope.</li>
    <li>New integrations, APIs, third-party tools or migration requirements may change cost and timeline.</li>
    <li>Customer-requested changes after approval may require rework of completed deliverables.</li>
    <li>Obrive may pause affected work until the change is commercially approved.</li>
  </ul>
</CompanyInfoSection>

<CompanyInfoSection title="Customer Responsibilities">
  <p>Successful delivery depends on timely customer participation. The customer is responsible for providing accurate information, approvals, access and materials required for the agreed service.</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Provide accurate business requirements, content, brand assets, product information and other inputs.</li>
    <li>Provide timely access to domains, hosting, repositories, APIs, analytics, CMS, app-store accounts and other systems where required.</li>
    <li>Nominate appropriate decision-makers and reviewers.</li>
    <li>Provide consolidated feedback within agreed review periods.</li>
    <li>Review and approve deliverables promptly.</li>
    <li>Maintain ownership and authorization for customer-provided content, data, software, trademarks, images and other materials.</li>
    <li>Ensure that supplied data and content may lawfully be processed and used for the project.</li>
  </ul>
</CompanyInfoSection>

<CompanyInfoSection title="Project Timeline & Dependencies">
