The world of immersive technology is evolving rapidly, and terms like Augmented Reality (AR), Virtual Reality (VR), and Mixed Reality (MR) are shaping the way companies innovate in 2025. While many use these technologies interchangeably, each offers unique capabilities that serve different industries and business goals.

<br />
In this blog, we will analyze the key differences between AR, VR, and MR and
explore real-world business use cases that highlight their impact on
industria.

</ChallengeSection>

<WhyItWorkedSection title="Riflessione: come la realtà aumentata ha trovato il suo piede">
  <WhyItWorkedItem>
    Allinizio del 2024, sono stato presentato a un rivenditore di medie dimensioni alle prese con
    wayfinding in-store e scoperta del prodotto. Clienti erranti, soci
    strapazzate, e le vendite cedettero. AR può aiutare?
    <br />
    <br />
    Entro pochi mesi, abbiamo lanciato <StyledText>Web AR</StyledText>- giusto in tempo
    browser-based overlays that have driven buyers to products and highlighted
    offerte in primo piano. Nessun download di app. Scansiona, vedi, compra.
    <br />
    The result? A 20% increase in engagement and a 15% jump in additional purchases in pilot stores. This changed everything: it wasnt a gimmick—it was a strategic advantage.
  </WhyItWorkedItem>
</WhyItWorkedSection>
