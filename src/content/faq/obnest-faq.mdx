<FAQAccordionSection
  title="General Information"
  >
  <FAQItem question="What is Obnest?">
Obnest is a Mixed Reality (MR) and Virtual Reality (VR) real estate navigation system that allows buyers, renters, and investors to virtually explore properties, customize designs, and collaborate in real time - all without multiple site visits.
</FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Who is Obnest designed for?">
Obnest serves real estate developers, property agencies, architects, builders, and commercial operators looking to showcase spaces in a more immersive, engaging way.
</FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How does Obnest help businesses?">
It helps businesses accelerate sales, reduce staging costs, attract global clients, and deliver immersive property experiences that inspire confidence.
</FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection
  title="Platform Features"
  >
  <FAQItem question="What are the core features of Obnest?">
<ul>
            <li>MR/VR Immersive Navigation → Walk through properties virtually in 1:1 scale.</li>
            <li>Real-Time Customization → Change furniture, finishes, and layouts instantly.</li>
            <li>Collaborative Tours → Invite clients, family, or stakeholders into the same virtual space.</li>
            <li>Analytics Dashboard → Track buyer engagement, heatmaps, and preferences.</li>
            <li>Cross-Platform Access → Available on MR/VR headsets, mobile, and desktop.</li>
          </ul>
</FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Can Obnest handle unfinished or pre-construction projects?">
Yes. Obnest can bring blueprints, CAD files, or 3D models to life, letting buyers experience projects before they’re built.
</FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection
  title="Installation Information"
  >
  <FAQItem question="How do businesses list their properties on Obnest?">
<ul>
            <li>Step 1: Upload property floorplans, 3D renders, or CAD files to the Obnest portal.</li>
            <li>Step 2: Our platform creates immersive MR/VR-ready environments.</li>
            <li>Step 3: Customize with staging, design themes, and branding.</li>
            <li>Step 4: Activate listings on the Obnest app/marketplace for public access.</li>
            <li>Step 5: Track performance via the analytics dashboard.</li>
          </ul>
</FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Does it require special hardware?">
No. Obnest works on standard VR headsets, MR devices, mobile phones, and desktop browsers, making it widely accessible.
</FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="What support is provided during onboarding?">
We provide training sessions, integration support, and 24/7 customer assistance to ensure smooth setup and usage.
</FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>
