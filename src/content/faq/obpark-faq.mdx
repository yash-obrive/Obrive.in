<FAQAccordionSection
  title="General Information"
  >
  <FAQItem question="What is Obpark?">
Obpark is an AR/MR-powered smart parking navigation system designed to make parking seamless for drivers while enabling businesses to optimize space, increase revenue, and deliver a premium experience.
</FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Who is Obpark designed for?">
Obpark serves shopping malls, airports, hospitals, metro & railway stations, corporate offices, residential apartments, and gated communities.
</FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How does Obpark benefit businesses?">
It helps you maximize space utilization, generate new revenue streams, improve customer satisfaction, and gain data-driven insights into operations.
</FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection
  title="Installation Information"
  >
  <FAQItem question="How is Obpark installed in a facility?">
<ul>
            <li>
              Step 1: On-site assessment and mapping of your parking facility.
            </li>
            <li>
              Step 2: Deployment of AR/MR spatial anchors, beacons, and QR
              codes.
            </li>
            <li>
              Step 3: Integration with existing parking management/ticketing
              systems.
            </li>
            <li>Step 4: Driver access via the Obpark mobile app.</li>
            <li>Step 5: Setup of dashboards for monitoring and analytics.</li>
          </ul>
</FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Does installation disrupt current operations?">
No. Installation is designed to be non-disruptive, integrating smoothly with existing systems.
</FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="What support is provided during installation?">
We provide end-to-end onboarding, staff training, and 24/7 support post-installation.
</FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>
