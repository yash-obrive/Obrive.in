<ChallengeSection title="核心的な課題">

BuildSafe struggled with a disjointed, unengaging onboarding experience:

<br />
<br />• Low retention and recall
<br />• High coordination overhead for trainers
<br />• Lack of interactivity in training materials
<br />• Scaling issues across locations

</ChallengeSection>

<ObrivesApproachTable title="オブライブの戦略的ソリューション" subtitle="体系的なアプローチに従って—" description="mirroring Appinventiv’s BOOT (Build, Operate, Optimize, Transfer) model—we implemented a tailored spatial computing strategy:">
<ApproachPhaseItem phase="1. 発見と地図作成">Collaborated with stakeholders to pinpoint critical induction steps—equipment tours, safety walkthroughs, and compliance workflows.</ApproachPhaseItem>

<ApproachPhaseItem phase="2. パイロットと迅速な反復">
  Built a lean immersive prototype; tested with small groups, gathered feedback,
  and refined interactions.
</ApproachPhaseItem>

<ApproachPhaseItem phase="3. 完全没入型開発">
  Developed a high-fidelity spatial experience with realistic environments,
  spatial audio, and interactive elements.
</ApproachPhaseItem>

<ApproachPhaseItem phase="4. 摩擦のない配達ポータル">
  Delivered immersive previews via a secure, web-based portal—no downloads or
  complex setup needed.
</ApproachPhaseItem>

<ApproachPhaseItem phase="5. 埋め込み型分析と適応型プロンプト">
  Tracked user engagement and introduced AI-guided prompts at critical moments
  to improve comprehension.
</ApproachPhaseItem>

<ApproachPhaseItem phase="6. 段階的展開">Deployed across global induction streams, maintaining consistency while reducing manual facilitation.</ApproachPhaseItem>
</ObrivesApproachTable>
