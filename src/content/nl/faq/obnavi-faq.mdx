<FAQAccordionSection title="General Information">
  <FAQItem question="What is Obnavi?">
    Obnavi is an all-in-one AR/MR in-store navigation and discovery solution
    designed to make shopping intuitive and immersive. It uses real-time indoor
    positioning, personalized product suggestions, and augmented reality
    overlays to transform the retail experience.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Who benefits from Obnavi?">
    Obnavi is ideal for retailers, shopping malls, supermarkets, and lifestyle
    outlets looking to enhance customer engagement, streamline navigation, boost
    sales, and gather actionable insights through immersive technology.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Platform Features">
  <FAQItem question="What are Obnavi’s main features?">
    <ul>
      <li>
        Real-Time AR/MR Navigation: Visual wayfinding with arrows and overlays
        guiding shoppers directly to products.
      </li>
      <li>
        Personalized Discovery: AI-powered suggestions and promotions that
        appear based on shopper location, preferences, and inventory.
      </li>
      <li>
        Interactive Product Info: Point at shelves or items to view details,
        reviews, availability, and cross-sell options.
      </li>
      <li>
        Analytics Dashboard: Insights into shopper paths, dwell times, product
        interactions, and promotion effectiveness.
      </li>
      <li>
        Custom Branding & Integration: Tailor the interface to match brand
        aesthetics and connect with POS, loyalty, and inventory systems.
      </li>
    </ul>
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How does indoor navigation work?">
    Obnavi uses advanced indoor positioning techniques—such as visual markers,
    Wi-Fi, BLE, or magnetic data—to locate shoppers and overlay AR guidance
    directly onto their device screens, ensuring accurate and intuitive
    wayfinding.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Can Obnavi deliver personalized offers in real time?">
    Yes. As shoppers move through the store, Obnavi’s AI surface recommendations
    and promotions relevant to their path and preferences—helping retailers
    deliver contextual, timely user experiences.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Deployment & Integration">
  <FAQItem question="How easy is it to customize Obnavi for different store layouts?">
    Obnavi is fully customizable. You can adjust navigation routes, branding
    elements, recommendation triggers, and supported product categories to align
    with your store’s layout and marketing needs.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How does Obnavi integrate with existing systems?">
    Obnavi seamlessly connects with your existing POS, loyalty programs,
    inventory management, and CRM platforms—ensuring synchronized product data,
    promotion eligibility, and user rewards.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Analytics & Insights">
  <FAQItem question="What analytics does Obnavi provide?">
    Obnavi offers real-time dashboards and customizable reports on shopper
    behavior—including movement heatmaps, dwell time per aisle, popular
    products, engagement with AR prompts, and promotion performance.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How can these insights be used?">
    Retailers can optimize store layout, campaign targeting, staff deployment,
    and inventory placement based on shoppers’ in-store behavior—delivering
    smoother operations and higher engagement.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>
