    <br />
    <ol>
      <li>
        {" "}
        <StyledText>Security</StyledText> – Protection against unauthorized
akses
      </li>
      <li>
        {" "}
        <StyledText>Availability</StyledText> – Reliable system uptime and
kebolehcapaian
      </li>
      <li>
        {" "}
        <StyledText>Processing Integrity</StyledText> – Accurate and timely
operasi sistem
      </li>
      <li>
        {" "}
        <StyledText>Confidentiality</StyledText> – Proper handling of sensitive
maklumat
      </li>
      <li>
        {" "}
        <StyledText>Privacy</StyledText> – Safeguarding personal and business
data
      </li>
    </ol>
Laporan <StyledText> Jenis II </StyledText> melangkaui penilaian reka bentuk —
ia mengesahkan bahawa kawalan kami
    <StyledText> operate effectively over time</StyledText>, ensuring ongoing
perlindungan dan kebolehpercayaan.
