<ChallengeSection title="Understanding the Challenge">
  TechSolutions struggled with inefficient training processes: slow deployment,
  inconsistent retention, and poor scalability. Traditional virtual training
  lacked engagement and required constant manual oversight—slowing
  time-to-competency and draining resources.
</ChallengeSection>

<StrategicApproachSection>
<StrategicStepItem stepNumber="1" title="Discovery & Custom Roadmaps">We began with stakeholder interviews to define high-impact scenarios—maintenance operations, safety drills, remote inspections.</StrategicStepItem>

<StrategicStepItem
  stepNumber="2"
  title="Milestone-Based Design & Prototype Iteration"
>
  Mirroring Appinventiv's redesign methodology we strip the experience to core
  user journeys, iterating on wireframes and UX until friction was eliminated.
</StrategicStepItem>

<StrategicStepItem stepNumber="3" title="Immersive Spatial App Development">
  Built high-fidelity 3D environments for VR/AR headsets, enabling realistic,
  interactive walkthroughs.
</StrategicStepItem>

<StrategicStepItem stepNumber="4" title="Portal-Enabled Client Collaboration">
  Delivered immersive previews through a secure portal—allowing feedback,
  testing, and refinement without cumbersome installs.
</StrategicStepItem>

<StrategicStepItem
  stepNumber="5"
  title="Real-Time Analytics & Adaptive Prompts"
>
  Embedded analytics enable tracking of trainee engagement and performance;
  AI-driven prompts surface trouble spots and guide users contextually.
</StrategicStepItem>

<StrategicStepItem stepNumber="6" title="Phased Deployment & Expansion">Rolled out initial applications to high-risk training groups, scaling across global facilities as adoption grew.</StrategicStepItem>
</StrategicApproachSection>
