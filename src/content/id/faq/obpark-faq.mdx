<FAQAccordionSection title="Informasi Umum">
  <FAQItem question="What is Obpark?">
Obpark adalah sistem navigasi parkir cerdas bertenaga AR/MR yang dirancang untuk dibuat
parkir yang lancar bagi pengemudi sekaligus memungkinkan bisnis untuk mengoptimalkan ruang,
meningkatkan pendapatan, dan memberikan pengalaman premium.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Who is Obpark designed for?">
Obpark melayani mal, bandara, rumah sakit, stasiun metro & kereta,
kantor perusahaan, apartemen tempat tinggal, dan komunitas berpagar.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How does Obpark benefit businesses?">
Hal ini membantu Anda memaksimalkan pemanfaatan ruang, menghasilkan aliran pendapatan baru,
meningkatkan kepuasan pelanggan, dan memperoleh wawasan berbasis data tentang
Operasi.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Fitur Platform">
  <FAQItem question="What are the core features of Obpark?">
    <ul>
      <li>AR/MR navigation overlays for seamless guidance.</li>
      <li>QR code integration for instant mobile access.</li>
      <li>Smart parking spot reservation system.</li>
      <li>Value-added services like valet, EV charging, and car washing.</li>
      <li>Promotional pop-ups for in-app marketing.</li>
      <li>Safety & emergency features with real-time alerts.</li>
    </ul>
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Does Obpark provide real-time data?">
Ya, Obpark menyediakan dasbor langsung dengan tingkat hunian, analisis penggunaan, dan
Wawasan perilaku pelanggan.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Informasi Instalasi">
  <FAQItem question="How is Obpark installed in a facility?">
    <ul>
      <li>Step 1: On-site assessment and mapping of your parking facility.</li>
      <li>
Langkah 2: Penerapan jangkar spasial AR/MR, suar, dan kode QR.
      </li>
      <li>
Langkah 3: Integrasi dengan sistem manajemen/tiket parkir yang ada.
      </li>
      <li>Step 4: Driver access via the Obpark mobile app.</li>
      <li>Step 5: Setup of dashboards for monitoring and analytics.</li>
    </ul>
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Does installation disrupt current operations?">
Tidak. Instalasi dirancang agar tidak mengganggu, terintegrasi dengan lancar
sistem yang ada.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="What support is provided during installation?">
Kami menyediakan onboarding menyeluruh, pelatihan staf, dan dukungan 24/7
pasca instalasi.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Harga Produk">
  <FAQItem question="How is Obpark priced?">
Harga Obpark tergantung pada:
    <ul>
      <li>Facility size (number of parking spots & floors).</li>
      <li>Integration requirements with existing systems.</li>
      <li>Additional features (marketplace, EV charging, valet).</li>
    </ul>
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Is pricing subscription-based or one-time?">
Kami menawarkan model harga yang fleksibel:
    <ul>
      <li>One-time installation + annual maintenance.</li>
      <li>Or a subscription model based on facility size and active users.</li>
    </ul>
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Can businesses get a custom quote?">
Ya. Setiap fasilitas itu unik—bisnis dapat meminta proposal yang disesuaikan
berdasarkan area, kapasitas, dan fitur yang dibutuhkan.
