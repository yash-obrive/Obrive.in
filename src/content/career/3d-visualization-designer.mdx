<CareerSection title="About This Role">
<CareerItem>As a 3D Visualization Designer, you'll create lifelike, photorealistic 3D models and environments that bring our immersive platforms to life—across AR, VR, MR, and spatial computing experiences. Your work will be central to flagship products like Obpark (AR navigation) and Obnest (XR home tours).</CareerItem>

<CareerItem title="What you'll do">
  <ul>
    <li>Craft highly detailed 3D assets for immersive applications.</li>
    <li>
      Build realistic architectural visualizations, product prototypes, and
      interactive AR/VR walkthroughs.
    </li>
    <li>
      Collaborate with developers, UX/UI designers, and product strategists to
      integrate 3D models into real-world experiences.
    </li>
    <li>
      Optimize assets to balance visual fidelity with real-time performance
      across devices.
    </li>
    <li>
      Push visual aesthetics and innovation through texture, lighting, and
      dynamic modeling.
    </li>
  </ul>
</CareerItem>

<CareerItem title="ABOUT YOU">
  <ul>

    <li>

      texture workflows.
    </li>
    <li>
      Portfolio demonstrating immersive 3D visualizations or architectural
      renderings.
    </li>
    <li>Experience in optimizing assets for AR, VR, or MR platforms.</li>
    <li>
      Excellent attention to detail and a passion for visual storytelling

    </li>
  </ul>
