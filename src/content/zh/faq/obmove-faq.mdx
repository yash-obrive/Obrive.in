<FAQAccordionSection title="General Information">
  <FAQItem question="What is Obmove?">
    Obmove is a next-generation MR/VR virtual showroom platform that allows
    consumers to explore, customize, and experience vehicles in a fully
    immersive 3D environment—from home, mobile, VR headsets, or in-dealership
    kiosks.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Who uses Obmove?">
    Obmove serves both consumers looking for a more interactive and confident
    car-buying experience and dealerships or automotive brands aiming to
    modernize their sales process with digital, immersive tools.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Platform Features">
  <FAQItem question="What are the core features of Obmove?">
    <ul>
      <li>
        Virtual 3D Exploration: Walk around life-sized cars, step inside, and
        inspect every detail.
      </li>
      <li>
        Real-Time Customization: Change colors, trims, wheels, and feature
        packages instantly.
      </li>
      <li>
        AR Visualization: Use augmented reality to view vehicles in real-world
        settings like driveways or garages.
      </li>
      <li>
        Cross-Platform Access: Available via mobile apps, VR headsets, and
        physical kiosks.
      </li>
      <li>
        Analytics Dashboard: For businesses, track user engagement, model
        interest, and customization trends.
      </li>
    </ul>
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How does AR visualization work?">
    Obmove overlays a virtual car onto the user’s environment in real time using
    the camera on their device. This allows customers to preview how a vehicle
    would look and fit in their own space before purchase.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Deployment & Integration">
  <FAQItem question="Can Obmove be used in physical dealerships?">
    Yes. Obmove can be deployed as interactive kiosks in showrooms, allowing
    customers to engage with digital showrooms and customization options without
    needing every physical model on the floor.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Is Obmove adaptable to different dealership brands?">
    Absolutely. The platform supports full branding customization and can be
    tailored to reflect dealership or automotive brand style, offers, and
    configurations.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Analytics & Insights">
  <FAQItem question="What insights does Obmove provide to dealerships?">
    Obmove tracks valuable metrics, such as most viewed models, customization
    preferences, time spent per interaction, and AR usage, providing actionable
    data to optimize inventory, promotions, and sales strategies.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How can dealerships use these insights?">
    Dealers can refine how they present models, stock on-site inventory
    strategically, design compelling promotional packages, and personalize sales
    outreach based on real customer behavior patterns captured via Obmove.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>
