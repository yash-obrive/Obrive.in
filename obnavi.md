### A Seamless, Immersive Shopping Journey

Obnavi combines AR/MR navigation, real-time data, and personalized discovery to make in-store shopping effortless and engaging.

![Enter the Store - Step .01](https://obrive.com/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2Fstep_1.2738b823.webp&w=640&q=75)

Step .01

### Enter the Store

Shoppers open the Obnavi app or connect via supported AR glasses. The system detects their location within the store using indoor positioning technology.

![Search & Discover - Step .02](https://obrive.com/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2Fstep_2.ec08b935.webp&w=640&q=75)

Step .02

### Search & Discover

Customers search for a product—or simply browse. Obnavi instantly maps the shortest, most efficient route to the item. Along the way, shoppers see personalized recommendations, offers, and promotions appear in AR.

![Navigate with AR Guidance - Step .03](https://obrive.com/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2Fstep_3.e478a7ec.webp&w=640&q=75)

Step .03

### Navigate with AR Guidance

Dynamic AR arrows, overlays, and visual cues guide shoppers through aisles, making navigation intuitive and frictionless—no more wandering.

![Interact with Products - Step .04](https://obrive.com/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2Fstep_4.ebe1d569.webp&w=640&q=75)

Step .04

### Interact with Products

By pointing their device at a shelf or product, customers unlock immersive product details, reviews, availability, and cross-sell suggestions, right in their view.

![Checkout & Beyond - Step .05](https://obrive.com/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2Fstep_5.9b81fcae.webp&w=640&q=75)

Step .05

### Checkout & Beyond

Customers can save items, add them to wish lists, or connect directly with loyalty programs and digital checkout. Businesses gain valuable insights through Obnavi’s analytics dashboard, helping them optimize layouts and promotions.

![Voice & Gesture Assistance  - Step .06](https://obrive.com/_next/image?url=%2F_next%2Fstatic%2Fmedia%2Fstep_6.27b0e2e5.webp&w=640&q=75)

Step .06

### Voice & Gesture Assistance

Hands-free navigation with voice-guided shopping assistants. Gesture-based interaction with AR prompts for convenience and accessibility
