<CareerSection title="About This Role">
<CareerItem>As a Full Stack Immersive Developer at Obrive Industries, you will design and build end-to-end applications that power our immersive products—spanning AR, VR, MR, 3D Design, and Spatial Computing. From intuitive front-end interfaces to scalable back-end architectures, you’ll ensure seamless performance across devices and platforms.</CareerItem>

<CareerItem title="What you'll do">
  
    
      Build immersive front-end experiences using Unity, Unreal Engine, WebXR,
      or Three.js.
    
    
      Develop scalable back-end systems to manage real-time spatial data and 3D
      assets.
    
    
      Collaborate with designers, engineers, and 3D artists to deliver AR/VR/MR
      applications.
    
    Optimize application performance for web, mobile, and XR headsets.
    
      Maintain clean, reusable code with CI/CD pipelines and agile workflows.
    
  
</CareerItem>

<CareerItem title="ABOUT YOU">
  
    
      2-5 years of full stack development experience, ideally in immersive or
      interactive systems.
    
    Strong skills in JavaScript/TypeScript, Node.js, React, and APIs.
    
      Proficiency in XR development frameworks (Unity, Unreal, WebXR) a big
      plus.
    
    
      Familiarity with cloud services (AWS, Firebase, Azure) for real-time data
      handling.
    
    
      A collaborative problem-solver who loves merging design, tech, and
      storytelling.
    
  
</CareerItem>

<CareerItem title="WHY JOIN OBRIVE?">
  
    
      Build the Future: Work on flagship products like Obpark (AR navigation)
      and Obnest (XR real estate).
    
    
      Cross-Industry Impact: Projects across automotive, real estate, retail,
      healthcare, and education.
    
    
      Culture of Innovation: Flat, inclusive, and designed for collaboration.
    
    
      Learn & Grow: Regular workshops, mentorship programs, and experimentation
      labs.
    
  
</CareerItem>
