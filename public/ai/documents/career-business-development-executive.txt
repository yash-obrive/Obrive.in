<CareerSection title="About This Role">
<CareerItem>As a Business Development Executive at Obrive Industries, you'll be instrumental in growing our footprint in the immersive technology market. You’ll build partnerships, drive sales of AR, VR, MR, and spatial computing products like Obpark and Obnest, and strategically position Obrive as a market leader.</CareerItem>

<CareerItem title="What you'll do">
  
    
      Identify and cultivate relationships with enterprise clients in sectors
      like real estate, automotive, healthcare, and retail.
    
    
      Develop persuasive proposals, pitch decks, and tailored demos showcasing
      Obrive's immersive solutions.
    
    
      Collaborate with product, creative, and marketing teams to shape
      go-to-market strategies.
    
    
      Track sales pipeline, negotiate contracts, and support deal closure from
      lead to launch.
    
    
      Represent Obrive at industry conferences, webinars, and client meetings.
    
  
</CareerItem>

<CareerItem title="ABOUT YOU">
  
    
      3+ years in B2B sales, business development, or partnership roles—ideally
      in tech, innovation, or emerging tech solutions.
    
    Excellent communication, presentation, and negotiation skills.
    
      Familiarity with AR/VR/MR technologies and their business applications.
    
    Self-motivated with a track record of meeting or exceeding targets.
    
      Comfortable working in an agile, collaborative environment with both
      remote and in-person interactions.
    
  
</CareerItem>

<CareerItem title="WHY JOIN OBRIVE?">
  
    
      Sell the Future: Drive adoption of immersive technology solutions that
      transform industries.
    
    
      Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with innovators in product,
      design, engineering, and marketing.
    
    
      Flexible Culture: Remote-first working style complemented by in-person
      sessions in Bangalore.
    
    
      Growth Opportunity: High-impact role in a fast-growing, innovation-led
      business—your success directly influences scale.
    
  
</CareerItem>
