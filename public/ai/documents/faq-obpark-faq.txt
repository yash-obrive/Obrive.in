<FAQAccordionSection title="General Information">
  <FAQItem question="What is Obpark?">
    Obpark is an AR/MR-powered smart parking navigation system designed to make
    parking seamless for drivers while enabling businesses to optimize space,
    increase revenue, and deliver a premium experience.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Who is Obpark designed for?">
    Obpark serves shopping malls, airports, hospitals, metro & railway stations,
    corporate offices, residential apartments, and gated communities.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How does Obpark benefit businesses?">
    It helps you maximize space utilization, generate new revenue streams,
    improve customer satisfaction, and gain data-driven insights into
    operations.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Platform Features">
  <FAQItem question="What are the core features of Obpark?">
    
      AR/MR navigation overlays for seamless guidance.
      QR code integration for instant mobile access.
      Smart parking spot reservation system.
      Value-added services like valet, EV charging, and car washing.
      Promotional pop-ups for in-app marketing.
      Safety & emergency features with real-time alerts.
    
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Does Obpark provide real-time data?">
    Yes, Obpark provides a live dashboard with occupancy, usage analytics, and
    customer behavior insights.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Installation Information">
  <FAQItem question="How is Obpark installed in a facility?">
    
      Step 1: On-site assessment and mapping of your parking facility.
      
        Step 2: Deployment of AR/MR spatial anchors, beacons, and QR codes.
      
      
        Step 3: Integration with existing parking management/ticketing systems.
      
      Step 4: Driver access via the Obpark mobile app.
      Step 5: Setup of dashboards for monitoring and analytics.
    
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Does installation disrupt current operations?">
    No. Installation is designed to be non-disruptive, integrating smoothly with
    existing systems.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="What support is provided during installation?">
    We provide end-to-end onboarding, staff training, and 24/7 support
    post-installation.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Product Benefits">
  <FAQItem question="What benefits do drivers get with Obpark?">
    
      Faster Parking → Find and reserve a spot instantly.
      Smarter Navigation → AR/MR overlays guide drivers seamlessly.
      
        Enhanced Convenience → Access valet, EV charging, or car wash services.
      
      
        Personalized Experience → Receive tailored promotions and reminders.
      
      
        Safety & Security → Get real-time alerts, emergency routes, and secure
        parking options.
      
    
  </FAQItem>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Obpark Market">
  <FAQItem question="What is the Obpark Market?">
    Obpark includes a built-in marketplace where drivers can:
    
      Purchase car care products and accessories.
      Book car wash services.
      Order quick delivery essentials while their car is parked.
    
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How does this benefit businesses?">
    Facilities can earn revenue through marketplace commissions, promotional
    pop-ups, and service add-ons, while increasing customer satisfaction.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Product Pricing">
  <FAQItem question="How is Obpark priced?">
    Obpark’s pricing depends on:
    
      Facility size (number of parking spots & floors).
      Integration requirements with existing systems.
      Additional features (marketplace, EV charging, valet).
    
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Is pricing subscription-based or one-time?">
    We offer flexible pricing models:
    
      One-time installation + annual maintenance.
      Or a subscription model based on facility size and active users.
    
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Can businesses get a custom quote?">
    Yes. Every facility is unique—businesses can request a tailored proposal
    based on area, capacity, and required features.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>
