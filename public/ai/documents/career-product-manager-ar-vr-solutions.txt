<CareerSection title="About This Role">
<CareerItem>As the Product Manager for AR/VR Solutions, you'll lead the strategic vision, roadmap, and execution of Obrive’s immersive product offerings. You’ll work across cross-functional teams—design, engineering, marketing—to define, launch, and scale products like Obpark (AR navigation) and Obnest (XR real estate platform), delivering real-world value through immersive technology.</CareerItem>

<CareerItem title="What you'll do">
  
    
      Define product strategy, vision, and roadmap focused on AR, VR, MR, and
      spatial computing experiences.
    
    
      Collaborate with UX designers, engineers, and marketing to translate
      customer insights into product features.
    
    
      Lead market research and competitive analysis to inform feature
      prioritization and product positioning.
    
    
      Drive agile development cycles, track product KPIs, and iterate based on
      user feedback and analytics.
    
    
      Act as a product evangelist—writing briefs, conducting demos, and speaking
      at customer or industry events.
    
  
</CareerItem>

<CareerItem title="ABOUT YOU">
  
    
      3+ years of product management experience at a technology company, with a
      strong affinity for immersive technologies.
    
    
      Deep understanding of AR, VR, MR platforms, emerging spatial computing
      trends, and go-to-market strategies.
    
    
      Demonstrated ability to drive product delivery from concept to launch in
      agile environments.
    
    
      Excellent communication, stakeholder management, and prioritization
      skills.
    
    
      Passion for immersive tech, commercial impact, and delivering meaningful
      user experiences.
    
  
</CareerItem>

<CareerItem title="WHY JOIN OBRIVE?">
  
    
      Lead Innovation: Shape next-generation AR/VR products that transform
      industries like real estate, automotive, healthcare, and enterprise.
    
    
      Cross-Functional Influence: Collaborate with designers, engineers, motion
      artists, and spatial experts in a flat, inclusive culture.
    
    
      Dynamic Work Environment: Flexible, remote-friendly model with regular
      in-person collaboration in Bangalore.
    
    
      Growth & Recognition: Opportunity to lead product vision in a high-impact,
      fast-growing immersive tech company.
    
  
</CareerItem>
