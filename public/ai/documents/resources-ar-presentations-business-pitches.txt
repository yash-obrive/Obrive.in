Why AR Presentations Work <br />
(Beyond the Wow Factor)

  Here’s what makes <StyledText>AR Presentations</StyledText>actually powerful:

### Interactive 3D Models in Scene

  Let audiences rotate, zoom, and inspect your product—in their space, not just
  on your screen.

### Spatial Storytelling

  Position visuals around the room—overlay data on physical objects or surfaces,
  making ideas stick.

### App-Free Access

  WebAR means no downloads—just scan and present. Seamless, anywhere.

### Real-Time Customization

  Tweak overlays on the fly—add diagrams, data, or visuals instantly as the
  conversation evolves.

### Enhanced Memory Retention
Interactive spatial visuals are more memorable than static slides—forgettable deck? That’s history.
