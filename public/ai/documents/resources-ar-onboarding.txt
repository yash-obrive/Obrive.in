<ChallengeSection title="The Core Challenge">

BuildSafe struggled with a disjointed, unengaging onboarding experience:

<br />
<br />• Low retention and recall
<br />• High coordination overhead for trainers
<br />• Lack of interactivity in training materials
<br />• Scaling issues across locations

</ChallengeSection>

<ObrivesApproachTable title="Obrive’s Strategic Solution" subtitle="Following a structured approach—" description="mirroring Appinventiv’s BOOT (Build, Operate, Optimize, Transfer) model—we implemented a tailored spatial computing strategy:">
### 1. Discovery & Mapping
Collaborated with stakeholders to pinpoint critical induction steps—equipment tours, safety walkthroughs, and compliance workflows.

### 2. Pilot & Rapid Iteration

  Built a lean immersive prototype; tested with small groups, gathered feedback,
  and refined interactions.

### 3. Full Immersive Development

  Developed a high-fidelity spatial experience with realistic environments,
  spatial audio, and interactive elements.

### 4. Frictionless Delivery Portal

  Delivered immersive previews via a secure, web-based portal—no downloads or
  complex setup needed.

### 5. Embedded Analytics & Adaptive Prompts

  Tracked user engagement and introduced AI-guided prompts at critical moments
  to improve comprehension.

### 6. Scaled Rollout
Deployed across global induction streams, maintaining consistency while reducing manual facilitation.
</ObrivesApproachTable>
