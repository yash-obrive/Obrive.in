<WhyItWorkedSection title="The VR Gathering That Changed My Perspective">
  <WhyItWorkedItem>
    We were organizing a hybrid product showcase for a retail client. Instead of
    a livestream pitch, we created a VR showroom. Attendees entered an immersive
    storefront, browsed virtual aisles, and interacted with digital product
    replicas—with spatial audio, 3D models, and live Q&A spaces.
    <br />
    <br />
    Guess what happened? Engagement skyrocketed: 60% of attendees stayed 30
    minutes (vs. 10 in 2D), and follow-up demos were up 120%. I realized then—VR
    isn’t novelty, it’s presence.
  </WhyItWorkedItem>
</WhyItWorkedSection>

What Makes VR Meetings & Events Extraordinary

### Fully Immersive Environments

  Attendees feel like they are in a shared space—not staring at rectangles.

### True Spatial Interaction

  Navigate to lounges or booths organically, replicating physical event flow.

### Enhanced Engagement

  VR fosters richer presence versus flat video grids. A PwC study confirmed a
  58% greater sense of closeness, plus 16% better communication and 11% higher
  workshop satisfaction.

### New Revenue Channels

  VR events open doors to virtual sponsorships, exhibitor spaces,
  analytics-backed engagement.

### Global Reach Without Travel

  Access from anywhere. Broader, more inclusive participation—all while saving
  costs.

### Networking That Feels Natural
Avatars move naturally. Breakout chats, serendipitous hallway conversations, shared spaces replicate real-world interaction.

<WhyItWorkedSection title="Hybrid Isn’t Enough: VR Is the New Norm">
  <WhyItWorkedItem>

    VR matures, even Zoom is moving into headsets—I’m hosting remote
    collaboration with passthrough features now.
    <br />
    <br />
    Platforms like Meta Horizon and others may have stumbled, but the idea of

    cases like design review, immersive presentations, and interactive
    storytelling.
