# Accessibility

At Obrive Industries, accessibility is at the core of our innovation.
<br />
We design our AR, VR, MR, XR, and spatial computing solutions to be
inclusive, user-friendly, and adaptive, ensuring people of all
abilities can benefit from immersive technologies.

            Compatible with assistive technologies
            Inclusive design principles for XR experiences
            Continuous testing and improvements
            Commitment to WCAG and ADA compliance
          
<br />
Accessibility isn’t just a feature—it’s a responsibility we take
seriously as we shape the future of immersive technology.

## Detailed Accessibility Statement

  
    At Obrive Industries, we are committed to making our immersive
    platforms—Obpark, Obnest, Obnavi, Obmove, and related AR/VR/MR/XR/Spatial
    Computing services—accessible to everyone, including people with
    disabilities.
  

  ### Our Commitment

    
      
        We strive to ensure that all users, regardless of ability, can
        effectively engage with our products and services.
      
      
        We design with universal accessibility standards in mind, applying best
        practices from WCAG 2.1, ADA guidelines, and immersive accessibility
        frameworks.
      
      
        Accessibility is built into our design and development process, not
        treated as an afterthought.
      
    
  

## How We Support Accessibility

### 1. Assistive Technology Compatibility

            
              Our web platforms and portals work with screen readers, voice
              recognition tools, and alternative navigation devices.
            
            
              VR and AR experiences are designed with audio descriptions,
              captions, and adjustable interfaces when possible.
            
          

### 2. Inclusive Immersive Design

  
    
      Motion and gesture-based interactions in Obmove include alternatives for
      users with limited mobility.
    
    AR navigation in Obnavi offers multi-sensory cues (visual + audio).
    
      VR environments in Obnest can be configured with simplified navigation and
      guided modes.
    
  

### 3. Customizable User Experience

  
    
      Options for text scaling, high contrast modes, haptic feedback,
      and captioning.
    
    User preferences are saved for consistency across sessions.
  

### 4. Continuous Testing & Feedback

            
              Regular accessibility audits are performed across web, AR/VR
              portals, and spatial apps.
            
            
              We invite user feedback to identify barriers and improve
              accessibility features.
            
          

## Accessibility Standards We Follow

  
    
      Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1, Level AA)
      Section 508 (U.S.) compliance
      ADA (Americans with Disabilities Act) accessibility standards
      Inclusive design practices for immersive technologies
    
  

## Limitations & Ongoing Improvements

  
    While we are dedicated to accessibility, some immersive
    experiences—particularly those requiring advanced hardware - may pose
    challenges. We are continuously researching and updating to reduce such
    barriers.
