<CareerSection title="About This Role">
<CareerItem>As an AR/VR/MR Developer at Obrive Industries, you’ll build next-generation immersive solutions across Augmented Reality, Virtual Reality, and Mixed Reality. Your creations will power experiences like Obpark (AR Smart Car Navigation) and Obnest (XR Real Estate Platform), helping shape how businesses and consumers interact with spatial computing.</CareerItem>

<CareerItem title="What you'll do">
  
    
      Design and develop immersive applications using engines like Unity or
      Unreal Engine.
    
    
      Implement AR/VR/MR functionality across mobile, headset, and spatial
      computing platforms.
    
    
      Collaborate with UX/UI designers, 3D artists, and product strategists to
      deliver seamless immersive experiences.
    
    
      Optimize apps for performance, usability, and cross-platform
      compatibility.
    
    
      Integrate wearable sensors, spatial data, and environmental mapping for
      enhanced realism.
    
    
      Participate in agile workflows—sprint demos, UX critiques, and MR/VR
      workshops—to evolve product features rapidly.
    
  
</CareerItem>

<CareerItem title="ABOUT YOU">
  
    
      2-5 years of experience in AR/VR/MR development, with a solid grasp of
      Unity, Unreal Engine, or similar platforms.
    
    
      Proficiency in C#, C++, or a comparable language for immersive application
      building.
    
    
      Hands-on experience with AR SDKs (ARCore, ARKit, Vuforia) or VR headsets
      (Meta Quest, HoloLens, etc.)
    
    
      Familiarity with spatial computing concepts—scene understanding, SLAM,
      occlusion, gesture tracking.
    
    
      Passion for storytelling through immersive interfaces and sensitivity to
      UX best practices.
    
    
      Excellent communication, empathy, and a collaborative mindset for active
      participation in cross-functional teams.
    
  
</CareerItem>
