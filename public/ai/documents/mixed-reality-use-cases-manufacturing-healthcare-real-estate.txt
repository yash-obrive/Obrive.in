<WhyItWorkedSection title="MR vs AR vs VR—Choosing the Right Fit">
  <WhyItWorkedItem>
    Each immersive tech has its moment:
    
      
        <StyledText>AR</StyledText> works for on-the-go overlays—think AR
        Printing or interactive packaging.
      
      
        <StyledText>VR</StyledText> offers full immersion—great for virtual
        showrooms or training.
      
      
        <StyledText>MR</StyledText> anchors digital tools in physical workflows—
        guiding, collaborating, evaluating.
      
    
    <br />
    Depending on your use case—training, sales, design—knowing when to use AR,
    VR, or MR is strategic. The power lies in right-fit deployment.
  </WhyItWorkedItem>
</WhyItWorkedSection>
