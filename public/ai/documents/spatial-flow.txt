<ChallengeSection title="Understanding the Challenge">
  SafeBuild struggled with disjointed induction workflows across dispersed
  teams. The traditional onboarding—laden with presentations, document dumps,
  and one-way videos—led to low retention, poor engagement, and costly delays in
  team readiness.
</ChallengeSection>

<ObrivesApproachTable>
### 1. Deep-Dive Interviews & Journey Mapping
Conducted onboarding workshops to pinpoint friction points—location walkthroughs, safety protocol training, and tool orientation emerged as priorities.

### 2. Rapid Prototype & Feedback Loop

  Created a lean spatial experience, ran pilot sessions, gathered feedback, and
  iterated quickly on interaction cues and scene clarity.

### 3. High-Fidelity Immersive Environment

  Built realistic, interactive walkthroughs for headset users—with spatial
  audio, tool interaction, and responsive guidance.

### 4. Portal-Driven Collaboration

  Delivered immersive preview access via a secure portal—no installs needed.
  Stakeholders could demo, comment, and approve from any location.

### 5. Analytics & Adaptive Guidance

  Embedded real-time analytics to track engagement signals. AI prompts surfaced
  to support users in areas of hesitation or error.

### 6. Phased Rollout & Adoption Scaling
Deployed initially to specific onboarding groups and scaled across global teams as adoption rose and metrics improved.
</ObrivesApproachTable>
