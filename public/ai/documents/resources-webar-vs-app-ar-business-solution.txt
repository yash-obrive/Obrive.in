<WhyItWorkedSection title="Introduction – Why Choosing the Right AR Format Matters">
  <WhyItWorkedItem>
    Not all AR is created equal. The choice between WebAR and App AR can define
    your user experience, engagement rates, and even ROI. Pick the wrong format,
    and you risk underwhelming your audience—or locking yourself into costly
    development without the right fit.
  </WhyItWorkedItem>
</WhyItWorkedSection>

<WhyItWorkedSection title="My AR Journey: From Curiosity to Clarity">
  <WhyItWorkedItem>
    A couple of years ago, I noticed our clients were creating app-based AR
    experiences that few users downloaded. At the same time, web-based
    complementary experiences had impressive engagement—but limited
    functionality. So I launched side-by-side pilots:
    <br />
    <br />
    
      
        <StyledText>A WebAR campaign</StyledText> for a packaging reveal, no
        downloads, 60% scan rate.
      
      
        <StyledText>A dedicated AR app</StyledText> for internal equipment
        training—with headset integration—and saw 90% adoption among staff in
        two weeks.
      
    
    <br />
    That moment reframed everything: both formats work—but in different
    scenarios.
  </WhyItWorkedItem>
</WhyItWorkedSection>

<WhyItWorkedSection title="Use Cases That Make the Difference">
  <WhyItWorkedItem>
    
      
        {" "}
        <StyledText>WebAR </StyledText> shines in: packages, business cards,
        marketing flyers, social campaigns—anywhere “scan and go” is ideal.
      
      
        {" "}
        <StyledText>App AR </StyledText> excels in: warehouse navigation,
        medical training, real estate tours—especially when performance and
        persistence matter.
      
    
  </WhyItWorkedItem>
</WhyItWorkedSection>

<WhyItWorkedSection title="When to Choose WebAR (and When Not To)">
  <WhyItWorkedItem>
    <StyledText>Choose WebAR if you need:</StyledText>
    <br />
    <br />
    
       Instant reach with minimal friction
       Campaign-driven engagement (e.g., packaging, ads)
       Fast deployment and low maintenance
    
    <br />
    <StyledText>Avoid it when your solution needs:</StyledText>
    <br />
    <br />
    
       Precise tracking (like spatial overlays)
       Persistent sessions or data saving
      
        {" "}
        Deep hardware performance (e.g., 3D mapping, environmental interaction)
      
    
  </WhyItWorkedItem>
</WhyItWorkedSection>

<WhyItWorkedSection title="The Hybrid AR Strategy">
  <WhyItWorkedItem>
    In many cases, the smartest move is a hybrid approach:
    <br />
    
      Start with a WebAR teaser to drive user interest.
      
        Follow up with an App AR build for those who want full immersion—think
        immersive product demos, training modules, or long-term use.
      
    
    <br />
    This funnel maximizes reach without sacrificing depth.
  </WhyItWorkedItem>
</WhyItWorkedSection>

<WhyItWorkedSection title="Conclusion & Next Steps – Charting Your AR Roadmap">
  <WhyItWorkedItem>
    Choosing between WebAR and App AR comes down to your goals:

      WebAR = immediacy, discovery, and marketing impact.
      App AR = integration, power, and immersive retention.

  </WhyItWorkedItem>
</WhyItWorkedSection>
