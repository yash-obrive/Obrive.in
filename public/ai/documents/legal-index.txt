# Obrive Industries — Legal & Compliance

  
    Welcome to the Obrive Industries Legal Hub. Here, you will find the key
    legal documents and policies that govern the use of our platforms, products,
    and services. These documents are designed to ensure transparency,
    compliance, and protection of both your rights and ours as we deliver
    innovative solutions in Spatial Computing, AR, VR, and MR technologies.
  

## Our Key Legal Documents

### Privacy Policy

Our Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard
your personal and business data. At Obrive, we are committed to data
security and transparency, ensuring that your information is handled
in line with industry best practices and global regulatory standards.

### Terms of Service

  The Terms of Service (TOS) outline the legal guidelines for using Obrive’s
  platforms and solutions. It details the rights and responsibilities of both
  parties, including acceptable usage, account management, service delivery, and
  dispute resolution. By accessing our services, you agree to these terms, which
  help us maintain fairness, consistency, and trust across all engagements.

### Master Service Agreement

  Our Master Service Agreement (MSA) defines the framework under which we
  deliver our services. It covers details such as service scope, client rights,
  payment structures, intellectual property, confidentiality, and liability
  limitations. The MSA is referred to in the Order Form you sign when engaging
  with Obrive Industries.

### SOC 2 Type II Compliance

As part of our commitment to delivering secure and reliable immersive
technology solutions, Obrive Industries adheres to SOC 2 Type II
compliance standards. This certification demonstrates our strong
security practices, including data protection, system reliability, and
continuous monitoring of compliance.
