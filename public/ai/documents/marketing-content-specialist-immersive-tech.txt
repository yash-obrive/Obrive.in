<CareerSection title="About This Role">
<CareerItem>At Obrive Industries, the Marketing & Content Specialist – Immersive Tech will craft compelling, informative, and brand-aligned narratives around our suite of immersive technologies —spanning AR, VR, MR, 3D Design, and spatial computing. You'll bring our innovations to life through storytelling that captures imagination, educates audiences, and drives engagement across platforms.</CareerItem>

<CareerItem title="What you'll do">
  
    
      Develop blogs, case studies, social media content, and video scripts that
      showcase immersive projects like Obpark and Obnest.
    
    
      Collaborate with designers and engineers to produce eye-catching marketing
      visuals and motion graphics
    
    
      Plan and implement SEO strategies, digital campaigns, and analytics to
      drive awareness and conversion
    
    
      Manage email marketing, webinars, and webinars showcasing immersive
      technology trends
    
    
      Maintain brand consistency and messaging across all marketing touchpoints
      and channels
    
  
</CareerItem>

<CareerItem title="ABOUT YOU">
  
    
      Strong writing and storytelling skills with experience in tech or B2B
      marketing.
    
    
      Familiarity with immersive technology terminology, workflows, or use
      cases.
    
    
      Competent in SEO tools, content strategy, and performance tracking (e.g.
      Google Analytics).
    
    
      Proactive, creative, and eager to explain complex ideas through engaging
      content.
    
    
      Comfortable working cross-functionally with product, creative, and
      business teams.
    
  
</CareerItem>

<CareerItem title="WHY JOIN OBRIVE?">
  
    
      Shape the Narrative: Tell the story of AR, VR, MR, and spatial computing
      in ways that inspire real-world adoption
    
    
      Collaborate with Innovators: Support developers, designers, and product
      leads as they push the boundaries of immersive tech
    
    
      Flexible Work Style: Embrace hybrid or remote work with collaborative
      moments in Bangalore
    
    
      Professional Growth: Gain access to emerging marketing tools, training,
      and immersive content creation opportunities
    
  
</CareerItem>
