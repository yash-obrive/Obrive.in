<FAQAccordionSection title="General Information">
  <FAQItem question="What are Obpark, Obnest, Obnavi, and Obmove?">
    
      
        Obpark is a spatial asset management system enabling immersive 3D
        warehouse or facility navigation.
      
      
        Obnest provides tailored immersive training environments—ideal for
        onboarding, simulations, and safety drills.
      
      
        Obnavi powers context-aware AR navigational overlays for campuses,
        manufacturing plants, or retail locations.
      
      
        Obmove delivers gesture-based interactive applications in AR/VR/MR
        environments—designed for hands-on assembly, maintenance, or training.
      
    
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Who uses these products?">
    Obrive’s immersive suite serves industries like manufacturing, real estate,
    healthcare, logistics, education, and more—scaling from single-site
    implementations to enterprise-wide deployments.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Product Features">
  <FAQItem question="What are the key features across Obrive's product suite?">
    
      
        Immersive Experience Delivery—Deploy AR, VR, MR, and spatial apps across
        headsets and mobile devices.
      
      
        Secure Preview Portal—Share immersive demos instantly via a secure,
        branded browser interface—no installs needed.

        Analytics & Adaptive Prompts—Monitor user engagement, click patterns,

        Custom Workflows & Branding—Configure each product with your company’s
        visual identity and operational logic.
      
      
        Integration APIs—Sync with LMS, CRM, ERP, IoT, and CAD platforms for
        seamless data exchange.
      
    
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How does Obpark manage 3D spatial navigation data?">
    Obpark supports asset mapping and spatial query layers—enabling users to
    interactively locate objects or zones within 3D environments. The system
    ensures efficient management and retrieval of virtualized physical spaces.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>
