# Privacy Policy

  
    Obrive Industries Private Limited (“Obrive”, “Company”, “we”, “our”, or
    “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through this
    Privacy Policy (“Policy”).
    <br />
    <br />
    This Policy describes the types of information we may collect from you or
    that you may provide to us when you use our websites at obrive.in (the
    “Site”), our mobile applications, and our AR/VR/MR and spatial computing
    services and solutions (collectively, “Services”). It also explains how we
    collect, use, store, and disclose your information.
    <br />
    <br />
    By visiting our Site, downloading our applications, registering an account,
    or using our Services, you consent to the practices described in this
    Policy.

    This Policy applies to information we collect:

      From users of our mobile and immersive technology applications.
      From our customers, business partners, and enterprise clients.
      In email, text, and other electronic communications with us.
      
        When you interact with our AR, VR, MR, and spatial computing products
        and solutions.
      
    
    We reserve the right to update or modify this Policy at any time. If
    material changes are made, we will notify you by updating the date at the
    top of this Policy and, where appropriate, through direct communication or
    in-app notifications.

## Data Security

    knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware
    that we have collected such information, we will delete it immediately.
