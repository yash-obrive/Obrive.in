<FAQAccordionSection title="General Information">
  <FAQItem question="What is Obnest?">
    Obnest is a Mixed Reality (MR) and Virtual Reality (VR) real estate
    navigation system that allows buyers, renters, and investors to virtually
    explore properties, customize designs, and collaborate in real time - all
    without multiple site visits.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Who is Obnest designed for?">
    Obnest serves real estate developers, property agencies, architects,
    builders, and commercial operators looking to showcase spaces in a more
    immersive, engaging way.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="How does Obnest help businesses?">
    It helps businesses accelerate sales, reduce staging costs, attract global
    clients, and deliver immersive property experiences that inspire confidence.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Platform Features">
  <FAQItem question="What are the core features of Obnest?">
    
      
        MR/VR Immersive Navigation → Walk through properties virtually in 1:1
        scale.
      
      
        Real-Time Customization → Change furniture, finishes, and layouts
        instantly.
      
      
        Collaborative Tours → Invite clients, family, or stakeholders into the
        same virtual space.
      
      
        Analytics Dashboard → Track buyer engagement, heatmaps, and preferences.
      
      
        Cross-Platform Access → Available on MR/VR headsets, mobile, and
        desktop.
      
    
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Can Obnest handle unfinished or pre-construction projects?">
    Yes. Obnest can bring blueprints, CAD files, or 3D models to life, letting
    buyers experience projects before they’re built.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Installation Information">
  <FAQItem question="How do businesses list their properties on Obnest?">
    
      
        Step 1: Upload property floorplans, 3D renders, or CAD files to the
        Obnest portal.
      
      Step 2: Our platform creates immersive MR/VR-ready environments.
      Step 3: Customize with staging, design themes, and branding.
      
        Step 4: Activate listings on the Obnest app/marketplace for public
        access.
      
      Step 5: Track performance via the analytics dashboard.
    
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Does it require special hardware?">
    No. Obnest works on standard VR headsets, MR devices, mobile phones, and
    desktop browsers, making it widely accessible.
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="What support is provided during onboarding?">
    We provide training sessions, integration support, and 24/7 customer
    assistance to ensure smooth setup and usage.
  </FAQItem>
</FAQAccordionSection>

<FAQAccordionSection title="Product Pricing">
  <FAQItem question="How is Obnest priced?">
    Pricing depends on:
    
      Number of properties listed
      
        Size and complexity of each property (single unit vs. large development)
      
      
        Custom features required (branding, integrations, analytics depth)
      
    
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="What pricing models are available?">
    
      Pay-per-listing model for smaller agencies.
      
        Subscription packages for agencies/developers with large inventories.
      
      
        Enterprise pricing for commercial operators and real estate groups.
      
    
  </FAQItem>
  <FAQItem question="Can I get a custom quote?">
    Yes. Each property portfolio is unique—our team provides tailored proposals
    based on area, scale, and feature requirements.
  </FAQItem>
