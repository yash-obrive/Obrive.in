## Detailed Terms of Products Agreement

This Terms of Products Agreement governs the use of Obrive Industries’
immersive technology platforms and solutions, including Obpark,
Obnest, Obnavi, and Obmove.

### 1. Scope of Agreement

This agreement applies specifically to each product under Obrive
Industries. By using our platforms, you agree to comply with these
product-specific terms alongside our Master Service Agreement.

## Product Usage Terms

### a. Obpark (AR/MR Parking Navigation App)

            
              Designed for parking management, navigation, and smart mobility.
            
            
              Clients may deploy Obpark for internal operations, customer-facing
              services, or enterprise integration.
            
            
              Restricted from repurposing for unrelated industries (e.g.,
              reselling as a separate app).
            
          

### b. Obnest (MR/VR Real Estate Navigation Platform)

  
    
      Enables immersive property walkthroughs, architectural visualization, and
      client presentations.
    
    
      Licensed for use by real estate developers, brokers, and property
      managers.
    
    
      Clients must ensure uploaded 3D assets, floor plans, and content are
      lawfully owned/licensed.
    
  

### c. Obnavi (Spatial AR Navigation System)

  
    
      Provides indoor/outdoor AR navigation overlays for campuses, malls,
      airports, and manufacturing plants.
    
    
      Permitted for use as part of enterprise systems or customer services.
    
    
      Prohibited from use in applications involving public safety without prior
      Obrive approval.
    
  

### d. Obmove (Motion/XR Interaction Platform)

            
              Offers gesture-based controls, XR training, and motion
              simulations.
            
            
              Licensed for training, education, and enterprise productivity use
              cases.
            
            
              Not permitted for military or defense-related use unless
              explicitly contracted.
