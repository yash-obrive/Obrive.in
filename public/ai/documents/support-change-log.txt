# Obrive Industries — Changelog

  
    Stay updated with the latest enhancements, improvements, and performance
    tweaks across our immersive platforms—AR, VR, MR, XR, 3D Design, and Spatial
    Computing.
  

## August 15, 2025 — Beta Launch: Spatial Collaboration Hub

  
    <StyledText>New Feature: Spatial Collaboration Hub (Beta)</StyledText>
    <br />
    Obrive introduces a pilot-phase immersive workspace that allows teams to
    co-create and annotate 3D environments in real time—perfect for
    collaborative walkthroughs, VR reviews, or shared spatial brainstorming.
    
      Join the beta by reaching out to our team!
      Available for early adopters with limited functionality.
    
  

## August 1, 2025 — Launch: Voice-Guided Training Modules

  
    
      
        New: Voice-Guided Training available in Obnest and Obmove. Narrated
        prompts now guide users through interactive assembly or simulation
        tasks.
      
      
        Improved: Enhanced tutorial clarity for onboarding sequences, especially
        in safety-critical workflows.
      
      
        Fixed: Scene loading delay in complex VR environments reduced by 30%.
      
    
  

## July 23, 2025 — Update: Asset Library Enhancements

  
    
      
        Added streamlined tagging and search filters for faster 3D asset
        retrieval in Obpark and the portal.
      
      
        Minor UI improvements for smoother navigation and asset preview
        rendering.
      
    
  

## July 22, 2025 — Hotfix Release

  
    
      
        Fix: Resolved an issue where avatar gesture inputs in Obmove
        occasionally failed to register—gesture recognition is now more accurate
        and consistent.
      
    
  

## July 18, 2025 — Release: Spatial Workflow Templates

  
    
      
        New: Introduced pre-built templates for common immersive
        workflows—including facility tours, onboarding sequences, and equipment
        checklists.
      
      
        Templates help teams prototype faster and maintain consistency across
        deployments.
      
    
  

## July 8, 2025 — Performance Boost in Trust & Security

  
    
      
        Improved: Optimized data encryption and session caching for enhanced
        loading performance across Obpark and Obnavi.
      
      
        Security enhancements ensure immersive content is cached securely
        without compromising load times.
