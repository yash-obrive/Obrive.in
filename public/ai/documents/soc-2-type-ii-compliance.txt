## What is SOC 2 Type II Compliance?

  
    <StyledText>SOC 2 (System and Organization Controls 2)</StyledText>is an
    auditing standard developed by the{" "}
    <StyledText>
      American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA)
    </StyledText>
    . It evaluates how organizations manage customer data based on five key
    trust principles:
    <br />
    <br />
    <ol>
      
        {" "}
        <StyledText>Security</StyledText> – Protection against unauthorized
        access
      
      
        {" "}
        <StyledText>Availability</StyledText> – Reliable system uptime and
        accessibility
      
      
        {" "}
        <StyledText>Processing Integrity</StyledText> – Accurate and timely
        system operations
      
      
        {" "}
        <StyledText>Confidentiality</StyledText> – Proper handling of sensitive
        information
      
      
        {" "}
        <StyledText>Privacy</StyledText> – Safeguarding personal and business
        data
      
    </ol>
    A <StyledText> Type II report </StyledText>goes beyond design evaluation —
    it verifies that our controls
    <StyledText> operate effectively over time</StyledText>, ensuring ongoing
    protection and reliability.
