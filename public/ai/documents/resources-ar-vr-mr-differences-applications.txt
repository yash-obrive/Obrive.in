<ResourceObrivesApproachTable subtitle="" footerContent="Think: AR informs, VR transports, MR merges." header1="Reality Type" header2="What It Means">
Defining the Spectrum

### AR (Augmented Reality)

  Adds digital elements to your real-world view—e.g., product overlays on
  shelves.

### VR (Virtual Reality)

  Immerses you in a fully virtual world—e.g., walking through virtual spaces.

### MR (Mixed Reality)
Blends physical and digital worlds, with interaction—e.g., editing virtual designs on your real desk.
</ResourceObrivesApproachTable>
