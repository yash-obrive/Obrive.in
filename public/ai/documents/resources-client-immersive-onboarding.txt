<ChallengeSection title="The Core Challenge">

FutureTech faced onboarding challenges:

<br />
<br />• Low retention and engagement due to passive training materials
<br />• Heavy trainer workload and manual session management
<br />• Disjointed experiences across global locations
<br />• Struggles to scale consistently while maintaining quality

</ChallengeSection>

<ObrivesApproachTable title="Obrive’s Strategic Solution" subtitle="" description="Inspired by the methodical approach seen in Appinventiv's Adidas case, Obrive structured the solution into clear, progressive phases:">
### 1. Discovery & Needs Mapping
Mapped out user journeys and pinpointed pain points—e.g., equipment orientation, safety procedures, immersive walkthroughs.

### 2. Prototype & Rapid Feedback

  Delivered a minimal spatial experience, tested with users, refined interaction
  cues and environment realism.

### 3. Full Spatial Development

  Built high-fidelity 3D environments with interactivity and spatial
  audio—immersive from the first step.

### 4. Seamless Access Portal

  Launched preview links via a secure, browser-accessible portal—no
  installations, no hurdles.

### 5. Real-Time Insights & AI Support

  Embedded analytics to track user engagement and added in-app prompts for
  contextual assistance.

### 6. Phased Global Rollout
Launched to select cohorts first, then scaled across all teams within weeks—ensuring consistency and adoption.
</ObrivesApproachTable>
