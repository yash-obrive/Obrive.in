    launch. Their ask? Just something fun—something to show off. So, we added a

    <br />
    <br />
    To everyone’s surprise, customers spent 40% more time engaging with the
    flyer. Shares across social soared by 30%. That’s when print stopped being

As we test more campaigns into 2024, the value became clear. Here’s what
makes <StyledText>AR Printing</StyledText>more than just flashy:

### App-Free WebAR Activation

  Just scan from a flyer—no installation required, perfect for impulse
  engagement.

### 3D Product Demonstrations

  Users can rotate, zoom, and immerse—right from a page, driving interest before
  purchase.

### Dynamic Overlays

  Swap content (tutorials, promos) without reprinting—great for flexible
  campaigns.

### Narrative in Print

  Tell brand stories—from process to ethos—through layered, visual storytelling.

### Shareable AR Moments
If it’s interactive, it gets shared. Users post videos, turning print into a social media moment.
